 ABOUT US
 Fresh 4 U is a specialized Egyptian fresh-produce export company dedicated to
 bridging premium Egyptian agriculture with European and international markets.
 With our core specialization in sweet potatoes grown on our own farms, we combine
 direct agricultural control with flexible external sourcing to deliver a diverse portfolio of
 superior fresh fruits and vegetables.
 Operating under international standards, our facility and farm operations are fully
 certified (GlobalG.A.P., GRASP, SMETA, and ISO) to ensure absolute food safety,
 ethical practices, and full traceability for European buyers.
 Our integrated operations—encompass farm sourcing, controlled post-harvest curing,
 precision washing, sorting, grading, and customized packaging—guarantee full
 compliance with international retail and wholesale specifications.
